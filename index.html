Draining Cylindrical Water Tank

Explore how the water level and volume change over time.

Water height: 3.0 ft


Water volume: 6.75π ft³

Step-by-Step Solution

1. Find the radius.
Diameter = 3 ft, so r = 1.5 ft.

2. Convert the water-level rate.
The water drops 4 inches per minute:
dh/dt = -4/12 = -1/3 ft/min.

3. Use the volume formula.
V = πr²h
V = π(1.5)²h = 2.25πh.

4. Differentiate.
dV/dt = 2.25π(dh/dt)
dV/dt = 2.25π(-1/3).

Answer: (D)
-0.75π cubic feet per minute

The negative sign means the amount of water in the tank is decreasing.
